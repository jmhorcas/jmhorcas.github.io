Led by Professor José Miguel Horcas (UMA), the session offers comprehensive training on UVL (Universal Variability Language), covering everything from its basic fundamentals to its advanced applications. Through a practical and informative approach, it demonstrates how to model the variability and constraints of a real product line from an authentic pizzeria menu. The event, open to students, researchers, and professionals, consists of a 60-minute presentation followed by a debate on the future of software engineering and the UVL language.'
